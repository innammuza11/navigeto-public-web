# TravelOS agency pilot landing page

## Positioning and availability

TravelOS by Navigeto: **Less admin. More journeys.**

Audience: independent travel agencies, tour operators, destination specialists
and B2B travel teams. Conversion: a personal demo request, not a paid signup.

The public page explicitly describes the agency subscription as a pilot in
preparation. It does not claim instant provisioning, verified agency isolation,
automatic availability, guaranteed speed, unlimited usage, subscriber counts or
commercial results. The interactive workspace uses clearly labelled sample data;
it is not a screenshot of a completed subscriber product.

Navigeto B2B services and agencies' own services are distinct. Subscription
charges are separate from travel bookings. The owner's fixed USD25 markup per
adult and chargeable child applies to Navigeto's internal-cost-based B2B offer;
that internal pricing rule is not exposed on the public marketing page.

Pilot price recommendation for discussion: USD49/month for three users initially,
with a possible USD79/month regular plan after validation. These are proposals,
not approved published prices or billing terms. Confirm users, modules, AI and
storage limits, onboarding and support before taking payment.

## Landing page and enquiry handling

- Page: https://navigeto.com/travelos
- Sharing image: https://navigeto.com/travelos/opengraph-image
- Primary CTA: Request a personal demo.
- Secondary CTA: WhatsApp the owner's published business number +94 75 331 0101.
- Existing partner login remains separate from pilot signup.
- General public enquiry subject: `TravelOS agency demo — {agency}`.
- Details tag: `request_kind=agency_software_demo`, `product=travelos`.
- Contact consent is required; no marketing subscription is automatically added.
- Existing public-enquiry receipt and CRM delivery services handle intake.
- A separate idempotency namespace keeps demo retries independent of travel
  enquiries. Only request identifiers, digests and receipts are stored locally.
- Success is shown only after a server receipt. A timeout can be retried with the
  same details. Starting a distinct request requires a clear in-page confirmation.
- Only bounded campaign labels are captured with the submitted enquiry. No
  arbitrary URL/query or referrer is copied. No advertising pixel is added.

## Pilot rollout and success measures

Start with existing interested agency relationships and a product demonstration.
Track demo requests, qualified agencies, demos held, pilot activations and the
number able to complete a sample quote and booking. Keep software demo enquiries
separate from travel-sales conversion reporting.

Before charging: verify agency data isolation, provisioning, user permissions,
saved cost/quotation revisions, branded PDF and voucher output, and the hand-off
between agency-owned and Navigeto-operated bookings. Verify the actual workflow
with pilot users before expanding promotion.

No campaign has been posted, no messages sent and no paid advertising budget
has been activated as part of creating this page and copy.
